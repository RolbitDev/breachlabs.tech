Approach

A disciplined build process designed to keep projects structured, testable, and easy to evolve.

01

Define

Confirm scope, success criteria, constraints, and the simplest version that can ship without cutting corners.

02

Build

Implement with clean structure and predictable conventions, keeping complexity under control.

03

Validate

Test functionality and edge cases, tighten UX, and remove friction before calling it ready.

04

Ship

Release progressively, document decisions, and prepare the system to grow without becoming fragile.
