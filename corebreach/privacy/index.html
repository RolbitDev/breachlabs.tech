BreachLabs Technologies / Corebreach

Privacy policy

Effective 27 October 2026 · Android version 1.0.1

Who we are

Corebreach is provided by BreachLabs Technologies, an independent software studio. Contact us at rolbitdev@gmail.com about the game or this policy.

What stays on your device

The game stores your commander name, progress, achievements, cosmetic choices, settings and saved game information on your device. You do not need an online account or a wallet connection to play. Corebreach does not provide cloud saves. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data can erase your progress. Android or device backup services may separately keep copies according to your device settings.

Diagnostics and advertising

In this release, Unity engine diagnostics, Unity Analytics, cloud crash reporting and advertising are disabled. Corebreach does not automatically send gameplay reports or the local commander profile to BreachLabs. The About screen can copy diagnostic details to your clipboard when you choose. Copying does not send them to us; you decide whether to include them in an email.

Email and feedback

If you contact us, we receive your email address and anything you include, such as your name, device details, screenshots or bug reports. We use this information to reply, investigate problems and improve the game. Our legal basis, where applicable, is our legitimate interest in providing support and maintaining the game. You may object to this processing. Please avoid sending unrelated personal information. Never send passwords, private keys or wallet seed phrases.

Keeping and sharing support information

We keep correspondence while it is needed to resolve a request, follow up on a reported issue or meet security and legal requirements. When identifying information is no longer needed, it can be removed while retaining the technical issue or fix. We do not sell your support information or use it for targeted advertising. We may disclose information where required by law or necessary to protect users and our services.

Email providers and international processing

The contact address uses Gmail. Google handles email delivery and storage and may process information outside your country. Its privacy information explains its processing and international-transfer arrangements: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Your own email provider also handles messages you send. Contact us if you need more information about the handling of your support correspondence.

Optional support on Solana

The game is free, with no ads or in-game purchases. The optional Support & Collaborate screen displays a public Solana address and QR code. Corebreach does not connect to your wallet, ask for wallet credentials or submit transfers. If you choose to send support through a separate wallet, that wallet has its own privacy terms. Solana transactions and wallet addresses are public and may reveal activity associated with the sender. We cannot delete blockchain records. Support provides no gameplay rewards or advantages.

Websites, the store and your device

Opening a website link sends you to your device browser. breachlabs.tech is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts; those services can receive ordinary web-request information such as your IP address. The Solana dApp Store, Android, Unity's services outside the game and any wallet you use separately follow their own privacy terms. We do not control their independent processing.

Your choices and rights

You can play without emailing us or sending support. You can remove local game information through Android's app settings, but this may permanently delete progress. For information held in support correspondence, email rolbitdev@gmail.com. Depending on applicable law, you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal information, and may object to processing based on legitimate interests. These rights can have legal exceptions. You may complain to your local data-protection authority, including the UK Information Commissioner's Office where applicable: https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/.

Children and support requests

We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through support requests. If you are under the age at which you can give valid consent under your local law, ask a parent or guardian to contact us. If you believe a child has sent personal information, please contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

Changes

We will update this policy if our practices change. New account services, connected wallets or data collection would require an updated notice. The effective date identifies this version. The current policy is available at https://breachlabs.tech/corebreach/privacy/ and inside the game under Settings, About, Privacy policy.
